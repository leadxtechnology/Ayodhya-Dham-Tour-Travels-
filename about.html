About Ayodhyadham Tour Travels

A locally rooted travel and taxi service at Tedhi Bazar, Ayodhya Railway Crossing, dedicated to welcoming devotees, families, and wedding guests with comfort and traditional hospitality.

Our Introduction

Grounded Hospitality in the Heart of Ayodhya

Ayodhyadham Tour Travels operates from Tedhi Bazar near the Ayodhya Railway Crossing (Pin – 224123), placing us at a convenient gateway for visitors arriving by train, road, or air.

Visiting Ayodhya Dham or organizing family travel requires punctual pickups, clean air-conditioned vehicles, and drivers who understand local temple routes respectfully. We arrange private sedans, multi-utility vehicles, Tempo Travellers, and wedding cars tailored to each traveller's schedule.

Travel Services

What We Arrange for Travellers

From short local temple darshan trips to multi-day regional circuits and wedding logistics, our services cover a complete range of road travel requirements.

01

Ayodhya Sightseeing

Local taxi bookings for Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir, Hanuman Garhi, Saryu Ghat, Nageshwarnath Temple, and Guptar Ghat.

02

Airport & Station Transfers

Direct pickup and drop coordination for Ayodhya Airport, Ayodhya Dham Junction, Ayodhya Cantt, and Lucknow Airport.

03

Outstation Pilgrimage Circuits

Round-trip and one-way outstation cabs to Varanasi, Prayagraj, Lucknow, Naimisharanya, Chitrakoot, and Gorakhpur.

04

Family 7-Seater & Touring Cabs

Spacious seating in Maruti Ertiga, Honda Elevate, Innova Crysta, and Kia Carens for families travelling with elders and luggage.

05

Tempo Traveller Group Tours

Coordinated group coaches for pilgrim mandalis, extended families, educational visits, and community yatras.

06

Wedding Transportation

Bridal sedans, VIP family cars, and multi-vehicle guest shuttle support for weddings across Ayodhya and nearby districts.
